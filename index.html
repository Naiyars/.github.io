ABOUT ME

I’m Naiyar Shaman, an engineering student focused on aerospace systems and mechanical design. I spend most of my time working on CAD, refining designs, or thinking through how systems actually come together beyond just analysis.

Outside of school and engineering work, I enjoy bowling, working on personal projects, and staying involved in technical competitions and NASA programs. I like building things that have a clear purpose and pushing ideas until they’re something tangible.

I’m especially interested in working on human space exploration systems — the kind of engineering that has to operate reliably in extreme environments and can’t afford to fail.
